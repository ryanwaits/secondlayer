# Keys and billing

One account key opens everything hosted: Index, Streams, your subgraphs and webhooks, and the archive. Prepaid credits pay for it.

## Your key

[Sign in](https://www.secondlayer.tools/login) with your email. Your first key is shown once on [your account page](https://www.secondlayer.tools/account), where you can create more. Set it as `SECONDLAYER_API_KEY` ([Introduction](/docs#get-started)); the SDK, CLI and MCP pick it up on the hosted URL.

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $SECONDLAYER_API_KEY" \
  "$SECONDLAYER_API_URL/v1/index/events?limit=5"
```

Your own box takes a different credential, the instance token: [Self-host](/docs/self-host#instance-token). Never put `sk-sl_*` in `INSTANCE_TOKEN`, or the hex token in `SECONDLAYER_API_KEY`.

## What it costs

Reads, live or history, priced the same:

| Rows delivered | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| First 1M rows per account per month | free |
| Past 1M rows/month | $5 per 1M rows, $2 per 1M past $50 in a month |
| `304` on `If-None-Match` for a finalized page | free |

Block headers (`/v1/index/blocks`) are free, never billed and never a `402`; you pay for event and transaction rows. Reads of your own subgraph tables are free. A hosted subgraph's indexing bills the event and transaction rows it reads.

The SDK sends `If-None-Match` for you. Its cache holds the last 64 pages in memory; pass `etagCache` to keep one across runs.

Past the free monthly allowance, a key with no balance gets a `402` (`insufficient_credits`) with a top-up link, before anything is served. A key still under the allowance, or with a balance, reads normally and is billed per row after the response.

Your hosted stack (it runs your webhooks and subgraphs) bills memory at a minimum of 0.5 GB, about $10/mo while it runs, plus storage. A stopped stack bills no memory.

## Spend cap

Set a monthly cap on [your credits page](https://www.secondlayer.tools/account/credits#cap). It counts what you spend in credits this month.

| At | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 80% of the cap | an email, once a month |
| the cap | reads past your free 1M rows answer `402 spend_cap_reached` until the next month; webhooks keep running |

Raise the cap on the same page and reads resume. Remove it and there is no cap; the prepaid balance is the only limit.

## Balance alerts

At a zero balance your hosted stack stops within minutes: webhooks stop delivering (events are held) and subgraphs stop indexing until you add credits; your tables are kept and it starts again on top-up. Two switches on [your credits page](https://www.secondlayer.tools/account/credits) email you first.

| Switch | Emails when |
| --- | --- |
| 7 days | runway drops under 7 days at your current spend |
| 2 days | runway drops under 2 days, and again if the stack stops |

A top-up that puts runway back over 7 days re-arms both.

## Archive credits

Bootstrap, backfill, and reindex from the [signed public archive](/docs/archive) draw credits per partition bundle fetched; verification is always free. `bootstrap` and `repair` show the price against your balance before anything is charged. Every partition arrives over a short-lived presigned URL and is digest-checked before a row is written. Your first six repair bundles each month are free. Packs are $10, $25, $50, or $100. The prepaid balance is the hard cap.

```bash
secondlayer credits buy --email you@example.com --pack 25
secondlayer credits balance
```

`credits buy` opens a one-time card checkout against [archive.secondlayer.tools](https://archive.secondlayer.tools). `credits balance` and metered bootstrap read `SECONDLAYER_API_KEY`.

Auto-refill is off until you set it: `secondlayer credits refill --below 5 --pack 25`.

## Webhook signing secrets

A **signing secret** (`whsec_…`) is per-webhook and verifies an incoming webhook came from your instance (`verifyWebhookSignature`); see [Webhooks](/docs/webhooks). Never in `Authorization`. Rotate with `secondlayer webhooks rotate-secret`.
